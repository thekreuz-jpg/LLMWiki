# Design Styles

A living list of landing-page aesthetics for the **landing-page-prompt-generator** skill.
Edit freely in Obsidian — add new styles, tweak descriptions, remove ones you tire of.

Format per line: `- **Name** (short description of what the style represents)`. Group styles under any `## Category` headings you like — the picker ignores headings and only reads the `- **Name** (...)` lines.

The skill reads this file both to pick a style at random and to recall what each style means
when writing the three-paragraph prompt. Not limited to this list — better-suited professional
styles may be substituted with a stated reason (see SKILL.md Step 1).

# Website Design Styles — Master List

A comprehensive reference of website design styles, combining an existing baseline list with an expanded set of popular and visually impressive additions.

## Existing/Original list of Styles

- **Neobrutalist** (raw, bold, confrontational with structured impact)
- **Swiss/International** (grid-based, systematic, ultra-clean typography)
- **Editorial** (magazine-inspired, sophisticated typography, article-focused)
- **Glassmorphism** (translucent layers, blurred backgrounds, depth)
- **Retro-futuristic** (80s vision of the future, refined nostalgia)
- **Bauhaus** (geometric simplicity, primary shapes, form follows function)
- **Art Deco** (elegant patterns, luxury, vintage sophistication)
- **Minimal** (extreme reduction, maximum whitespace, essential only)
- **Flat** (no depth, solid colors, simple icons, clean)
- **Material** (Google-inspired, cards, subtle shadows, motion)
- **Neumorphic** (soft shadows, extruded elements, tactile)
- **Monochromatic** (single color variations, tonal depth)
- **Scandinavian** (hygge, natural materials, warm minimalism)
- **Japandi** (Japanese-Scandinavian fusion, zen meets hygge)
- **Dark Mode First** (designed for dark interfaces, high contrast elegance)
- **Modernist** (clean lines, functional beauty, timeless)
- **Organic/Fluid** (flowing shapes, natural curves, sophisticated blob forms)
- **Corporate Professional** (trust-building, established, refined)
- **Tech Forward** (innovative, clean, future-focused)
- **Luxury Minimal** (premium restraint, high-end simplicity)
- **Neo-Geo** (refined geometric patterns, mathematical beauty)
- **Kinetic** (motion-driven, dynamic but controlled)
- **Gradient Modern** (sophisticated color transitions, depth through gradients)
- **Typography First** (type as the hero, letterforms as design)
- **Metropolitan** (urban sophistication, cultural depth)
## 🎮 Nostalgic & Pop Culture

- **Retro Gaming** (pixel art, 8/16-bit, chunky UI, arcade energy)
- **Comic Book** (Ben-Day dots, halftones, bold panels, action lettering)
- **Pop Art** (Warhol-inspired, saturated blocks, repetition, high contrast)
- **Vaporwave** (pastel neon, Greco-Roman busts, glitch, '90s mall aesthetic)
- **Y2K / Chrome** (metallic gradients, bubble type, lens flares, early-2000s tech optimism)
- **Cyberpunk** (neon-on-black, glitch, HUD overlays, dystopian tech)
- **Vintage Americana** (retro diner, distressed signage, 50s–70s branding)
- **Psychedelic** (warped type, kaleidoscopic color, 60s/70s flow)
- **Memphis** (80s postmodern squiggles, confetti shapes, clashing color)

## 🎨 Illustrative & Handcrafted

- **Hand-drawn / Sketch** (doodles, imperfect lines, marker/pencil texture)
- **Collage / Cut-out** (mixed media, torn paper, scrapbook layering)
- **Watercolor** (soft washes, organic bleed, artisanal warmth)
- **Line Art** (single-weight outlines, minimal illustration, elegant restraint)
- **Corporate Memphis / Flat Illustration** (blob people, friendly startup vibe)
- **Naive / Childlike** (playful, wobbly, intentionally unpolished)
- **Zine / DIY** (photocopied, punk, raw and rebellious)

---

## 🌌 Immersive & Dimensional

- **3D / WebGL Immersive** (Spline/Three.js scenes, interactive depth)
- **Claymorphism** (puffy 3D clay elements, soft rounded tactility)
- **Skeuomorphism** (realistic textures mimicking real-world objects)
- **Isometric** (angled 3D-on-2D scenes, technical yet playful)
- **Aurora / Mesh Gradient** (flowing atmospheric color fields, ethereal glow)
- **Parallax Storytelling** (scroll-driven narrative, layered motion)
- **Cosmic / Celestial** (space themes, starfields, nebula gradients)

---

## 🕶️ Edgy & Atmospheric

- **Brutalist (Web)** (raw HTML, exposed structure, anti-polish — distinct from Neobrutalist)
- **Grunge** (distressed textures, ink splatter, gritty overlays)
- **Terminal / Hacker** (monospace, command-line, Matrix green-on-black)
- **Neon Noir** (moody dark scenes with neon accents, cinematic)
- **Anti-Design** (deliberately "wrong," chaotic, clashing — rule-breaking on purpose)
- **Steampunk** (Victorian mechanical, brass, gears, ornate industrial)

---

## 🌿 Thematic & Lifestyle

- **Maximalism** (more-is-more, dense layers, bold pattern-on-pattern)
- **Cottagecore / Botanical** (florals, soft naturals, romantic pastoral)
- **Dark Academia** (scholarly, moody, serif-heavy, vintage library)
- **Solarpunk** (optimistic eco-futurism, greenery + clean tech)
- **Mid-Century Modern** (60s furniture-era palettes, atomic shapes)
- **Kawaii** (cute mascots, rounded pastels, playful charm)

---

## 📐 Structural & Trend-Forward

- **Bento Grid** (modular boxed layout, Apple-inspired, highly current)
- **Duotone** (two-color overlays, striking and cohesive)
- **Grainy / Textured** (noise overlays adding warmth and depth to flat design)
- **Blueprint / Technical** (schematic lines, grid paper, engineering aesthetic)
- **Newspaper / Print Editorial** (columns, mastheads — a grittier cousin to Editorial)
- **Anaglyph / Glitch** (RGB-split, digital distortion as a decorative device)
